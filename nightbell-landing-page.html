How it works

Three steps, one line.

Nightbell sits behind your existing phone number. It only ever picks up the calls you don't.

01

A call comes in

Someone rings your shop. If you're with a customer, mid-shift, or closed for the night, Nightbell catches it instead of voicemail.

02

Nightbell texts back

Within seconds, the caller gets a text — written in your shop's tone, knowing your hours, your menu or services, and what you'd actually say.

03

They get handled

Booked, reminded, or answered — the conversation ends with the customer taken care of, and a note waiting for you in the morning.
